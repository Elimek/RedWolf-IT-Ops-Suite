# Value Proposition

> Quantified business value of the RedWolf IT Ops Suite

---

## Direct Cost Savings

### Manual Ticket Triage
- **Before**: IT staff manually reads and categorizes each ticket (~15 min/ticket)
- **After**: AI classifier categorizes in < 3 seconds (~30 sec/ticket)
- **Volume**: ~30 tickets/day average
- **Savings**: 14.5 min/ticket x 30 tickets x 22 working days = **160 hours/month**
- **Annual Value**: HK$192,000 (at HK$1,200/month for IT staff time)

### Server Monitoring
- **Before**: Manual checking or reactive response (downtime discovered hours later)
- **After**: Real-time monitoring with automatic alerting
- **Impact**: Reduced MTTD (Mean Time To Detection) from 2 hours to 3 minutes
- **Estimated downtime prevented**: 4 hours/month
- **Annual Value**: HK$120,000 (at HK$2,500/hour business cost of downtime)

### Office IT Support
- **Before**: Walk-up support for printer, network, VPN issues (~30 min/issue)
- **After**: Self-service tools + pre-diagnostics (~5 min/issue)
- **Volume**: ~10 issues/day
- **Savings**: 25 min/issue x 10 issues x 22 days = **91 hours/month**
- **Annual Value**: HK$109,200

## Intangible Benefits

1. **Improved Response Time**: Employees get faster IT support, increasing satisfaction
2. **Knowledge Retention**: Diagnostic procedures are codified, not dependent on individual staff
3. **Security Posture**: Audit logging and access controls improve compliance
4. **Scalability**: System handles growth without proportional increase in IT staff
5. **Data Privacy**: On-premise AI ensures customer data never leaves the network
6. **24/7 Visibility**: Monitoring runs continuously, even outside business hours
7. **Consistency**: Automated processes reduce human error
